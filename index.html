Featured coin / Roman Empire

An Emperor Taken Captive

Turn this coin over and you’ll see Valerian holding a globe, hailed as the restorer of humankind. He and his son Gallienus ruled together during one of Rome’s most turbulent decades. In AD 260, the Sasanian king Shapur I captured Valerian near Edessa.
